### 2. **Attribution & Credit System**

#### How External Links Are Handled (SEO-Safe)
✅ **Your pages are indexed** - All content is on your domain
✅ **Original sources get credit** - Clear attribution section
✅ **No SEO penalty** - Using `rel="noopener nofollow"` on external links

#### Attribution Section on Each Article
```html
<div class="attribution-section">
  <h3>📰 Article Source & Attribution</h3>
  <p>Originally published on <a href="{original}" rel="noopener nofollow">renewables.az</a></p>
  <p>Plugin.az curates renewable energy news from trusted sources...</p>
  <p><strong>Read original:</strong> <a href="{link}" rel="noopener nofollow">{link}</a></p>
</div>
```

**Why this works:**
- `rel="nofollow"` tells search engines not to pass SEO value to external site
- `rel="noopener"` prevents security issues
- Clear attribution gives ethical credit
- Your content is still indexed and ranked
